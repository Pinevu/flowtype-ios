# Third-Party Data & Licenses

The bundled `FlowTypeKeyboard/Resources/lexicon.sqlite` database is compiled by `scripts/build_lexicon.py` from the following MIT-licensed open-source datasets:

1. **mozillazg/pinyin-data** (MIT License)
   - Source: https://github.com/mozillazg/pinyin-data
   - Used for Chinese character-to-Pinyin readings.

2. **mozillazg/phrase-pinyin-data** (MIT License)
   - Source: https://github.com/mozillazg/phrase-pinyin-data
   - Used for multi-character Chinese phrase Pinyin readings.

3. **fxsjy/jieba** (MIT License)
   - Source: https://github.com/fxsjy/jieba
   - Used for Chinese word/character frequency ranking.

4. **hermitdave/FrequencyWords** (MIT License)
   - Source: https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
   - Used for English word frequency ranking (`en_50k.txt`).
